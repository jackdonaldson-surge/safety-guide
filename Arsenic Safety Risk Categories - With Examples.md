### General Notes: {#general-notes:}

- Mentions of content that falls within any of the below categories should still be annotated as a risk even in cases that include but are **not limited to**:  
  - Academic discussions that do not endorse the content or ideas  
  - Content intended to make note of topics or discussions to avoid, such as a workplace training video describing inappropriate language or behavior  
  - Historical texts including content or terms no longer considered politically correct or appropriate   
  - Discussions surrounding existing policy or the creation of policies
